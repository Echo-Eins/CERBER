**The conflicts:**

1. **MDSM vs RANK**: MDSM trains the energy gradient: `∇_v E(q, v, σ) ≈ (noisy - clean) / σ²`. This is **first-order gradient matching**. But RANK enforces absolute energy ordering: `E(clean) < E(actor) < E(hard)`. These can conflict because:

   - MDSM only cares about gradient **direction** and magnitude at noisy points

   - RANK cares about absolute energy values at clean points

   - The energy function can have zero gradient at certain points while still failing ranking

2. **CLEAN_MIN_PENALTY vs RANK**: 

   - `clean_min_penalty = relu(E_clean - E_actor + margin)` ← forces E_clean < E_actor

   This penalizes **all positive energies** without discrimination. Combined with rank loss:

4. **DIRECTION (redundant) vs MDSM**:
